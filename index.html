CASE STUDY

Gifted Hands Art Camp

Founder & Lead Instructor — Nyamata, Rwanda

THE PROBLEM

Young people in Nyamata had no structured, affordable space to build real artistic skill over the summer. There was no program, no curriculum, and no venue — just an idea and an audience that needed reaching.

WHAT I BUILT

  • A 4-week, 12-session curriculum for ages 8–17: foundational drawing → color theory → a final student showcase
  • A ~1M RWF budget, planned and managed end to end
  • Promotional flyers designed around persuasion principles
  • Outreach letters that secured a school as the venue partner

THE OUTCOME

A camp that ran from the ground up and ended in a student showcase — demonstrating project ownership, budgeting, curriculum design, and community outreach. The same muscles that product and project management need in tech.
